About Me

Physician. HSE Leader. AI Strategist.

For over 20 years, I have viewed Health, Safety and Environment not just as a set of standards, but as a practice dedicated to one core principle: protecting and enhancing human life.

01 The story

From Medicine to Industry

My journey began as a Medical Doctor (MD), graduating from Shivaji University in 2000. I spent nearly a decade in private practice, developing strong emergency response, analytical, governance and risk decision-making capabilities. These skills proved directly transferable when I transitioned into Health, Safety and Environment leadership.

The Intersection of HSE & AI

Today, I stand at the intersection of deep domain expertise in HSE and the transformative potential of Artificial Intelligence. My mission is to leverage AI, machine learning and intelligent automation to move beyond reactive compliance and build truly predictive safety cultures.

  • How can predictive analytics identify and mitigate risks before incidents occur?
  • How can automation free human experts for strategic safety initiatives?
  • How can data-driven insight create healthier, more sustainable work environments?

Global Experience, Regional Impact

Living and working in the dynamic landscape of the UAE has reinforced my belief in ambitious innovation. From offshore drilling operations in Angola to manufacturing facilities across the Middle East, Caspian, Africa and India — I have led HSE governance at scale. I am passionate about building teams that are not afraid to challenge the status quo.
